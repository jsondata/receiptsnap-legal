ReceiptSnap Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Receipt data

ReceiptSnap stores receipt images and expense details in its local app storage. Text recognition and PDF generation run on your device. The developer does not receive your receipts or expense details. The app has no account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or tracking.

Camera, photos, and sharing

The camera is used when you choose to scan a receipt. ReceiptSnap can read only the images you select in the system photo picker. You can revoke camera access in iOS Settings. When you share a PDF, the destination you choose receives a copy and handles it according to its own privacy policy. Apple handles App Store payments and device backups according to your Apple settings.

Retention and deletion

Saved receipts are kept until you delete them or delete the app. To remove a receipt, open it and tap Delete receipt. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Copies you exported, copies sent to other people, and device backups are managed separately.

Contact

For privacy questions, use ReceiptSnap Support. Please omit receipt images and personal details from public support requests.
