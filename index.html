ReceiptSnap Support

Scan and organize receipts on your iPhone, then export an annual PDF with your expense summary and receipt images.

Save a receipt

Tap Scan receipt or Import a photo. Review the merchant, date, total, and currency against the original image. Fill in any missing fields and tap Save.

Export or delete receipts

Tap Tax PDF, choose a calendar year if prompted, then select a destination in the share sheet. To delete a receipt, open it and tap Delete receipt.

Contact support

Open a ReceiptSnap support request. Include your iOS version and what happened. Requests are public. Leave out receipt images and personal information.
